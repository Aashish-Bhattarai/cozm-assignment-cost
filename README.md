# Assignment Cost Estimate: Switzerland → UK

A small tool that estimates what it costs an employer to send an employee from Switzerland to the UK for two years, and what the employee needs for UK immigration.

- **Tab 1, cost estimate:** total employer cost per year under tax equalisation, with the UK tax-on-tax gross-up solved by iteration.
- **Tab 2, immigration:** work permit, business visit, passport, company duties, timelines and fees.

Every tax rule and immigration requirement links to its official source. The decisions and their sources are recorded in [docs/decision-ledger.md](docs/decision-ledger.md).

Back end: Python + Django (`backend/`). Front end: React (`frontend/`).

Setup, test and AI-usage sections are added as the build progresses.
